# Marketing Strategy Overview

The company will focus on three primary channels: product education, customer advocacy, and lifecycle email campaigns. The goal is to generate demand while preserving a lightweight and efficient cost structure.

The marketing strategy emphasizes segmented content for SMB, mid-market, and enterprise buyers. Each segment receives tailored messaging and proof points based on vertical use cases, customer success stories, and pricing transparency.

The core KPI dashboard includes pipeline generated, conversion by segment, CAC payback, and sales-assisted content engagement. These metrics will support rapid iteration and personalisation.
